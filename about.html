About Taskly

Who we are

Taskly is a simple task manager built for people who want to stay on top of their day without fighting with complicated software. Whether you are a student juggling assignments and revision, or a team lead keeping a group on track, Taskly gives you one clear place to see what needs doing and when.

Our mission

To make planning easy. We believe a task list should take seconds to update, not minutes, so you spend your time doing the work instead of organising it.

What you can do with Taskly

  • Add tasks with a title, description and due date.
  • Tick off tasks as you complete them.
  • See your day at a glance in a clean, easy-to-read list.
  • Use it on your own or share tasks with others.

Who it is for

Individuals use Taskly to plan study, work and personal errands. Teams and organisations use it to assign tasks and keep everyone clear on who is doing what.
