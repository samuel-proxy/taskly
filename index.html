Plan your tasks once, get reminded on time

Taskly is a simple to-do web app that helps you capture tasks, set due dates and reminders, and tick them off as you go. Whether you are juggling classes, work or a busy household, everything lives in one clear list.

Taskly is for students, professionals and small teams. You only need an email address to sign up, and you can start adding tasks from the moment your account is created.

What you can do

  • Create and organise tasks
  • Set due dates and reminders
  • Assign tasks to teammates
